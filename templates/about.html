About

Built by someone who had to maintain it afterwards

Agentic AI Automation was built by our founder: eleven years in enterprise IT, nine of them building RPA for banks, hospitals, law firms and logistics operators across India, the UK and the Middle East.

Most of those nine years were spent on the unglamorous half of automation: keeping bots alive after the consultants left. That is where the opinions on this site come from. A bot that works in a demo and dies the first time a vendor changes a login screen is not automation, it is a liability with a project code.

How that shapes the work

We scope narrow. One process, one price, one date. Broad transformation programmes are where budgets go to die quietly over eighteen months.

We say no. If discovery shows the process is too unstable to automate, or that a ₹2,000-a-month tool already does it, we say so on the call. That has cost us work and it is still the right trade.

We build for handover from day one. Your repository, your cloud accounts, your credentials. Documentation and a runbook are deliverables, not an upsell. If you cannot fire us without the automation breaking, we designed it badly.

What we are not

Not a staffing agency, not a reseller, and not a fifty-person shop. Small team, senior people, a handful of engagements at a time. If you need thirty developers next month, we are the wrong call and we will tell you so in the first ten minutes.

That shows up in the detail elsewhere on this site: every one of the six services lists who it is wrong for, the industry guide names what we stay out of in each sector, and the case studies page says plainly that there is nothing published yet rather than filling itself with anonymous numbers.
