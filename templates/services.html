Straight answers

What clients ask before they commit.

Who pays for the AI model usage?

You do, directly to the provider. You use your own AI API key and receive your own bill — we take no margin on tokens and we do not resell usage.

Who owns the code when the project ends?

You do. Code goes into your repository, on your cloud, with your credentials, from day one — not at handover.

Whose WhatsApp number and Meta account are they?

Yours. The WhatsApp number and the Meta Business Manager stay in your name throughout, so nothing is stranded with us if you stop working with us.

What happens after handover?

Documentation and a runbook are standard deliverables, and thirty days of defect fixing is included. Ongoing support is optional by design, never a dependency.

Will you ever tell us not to automate something?

Yes, and we turn work down when maintenance would cost more than the saving. You get that answer on the free audit call rather than after the invoice.

Is the n8n instance metered per task?

No. We self-host n8n on your infrastructure, so there is no per-task metering from us and your bill does not climb with volume.
