Compliance

What your compliance team will ask, and the honest answer

UK buyers run automation past legal before it goes near customer data. The relevant regime is the UK GDPR alongside the Data Protection Act 2018, regulated by the ICO. The questions that follow are always the same three.

01Where is the data processed, and by whom?In your own cloud tenancy, under your own credentials. We do not host your data. Self-hosted n8n runs on your infrastructure, and the AI model calls use your own API key and your own bill — so the processor relationship is between you and the model provider, not routed through us.
02Who are the subprocessors?For the build itself, none that you have not chosen. The code lives in your repository from day one. That is deliberate, and it is the same answer we give every client regardless of market.
03What happens to the WhatsApp number?It stays in your name, in your Meta Business Manager, throughout. Nothing is stranded with us if you stop working with us.

None of that is a claim to be UK GDPR compliant on your behalf — no vendor can make that claim for you. It is a description of how the work is structured, so your own assessment has something concrete to assess.
