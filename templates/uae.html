Compliance

What your compliance team will ask, and the honest answer

The UAE's federal regime is Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (the PDPL). If you operate inside the DIFC or ADGM free zones, those have their own separate data protection regulations and your counsel will tell you which applies. The practical questions are the same either way.

01Where does the data physically sit?Wherever you choose. Everything runs in your own cloud tenancy under your own credentials, so the region is your decision, not ours. Self-hosted n8n runs on your infrastructure. If a build needs to stay in-region, it stays in-region.
02Does our data leave the country to reach your team?We access your systems to build and support them, and that access is scoped to the specific systems a workflow needs, with every action logged. We do not take copies of your data onto our own infrastructure.
03Whose AI provider account is used?Yours. Your own API key, your own bill, no margin taken by us. That keeps the model provider's terms a matter between you and them.

This is a description of how the work is structured, not a claim to be PDPL compliant on your behalf. Residency and compliance questions are settled at scoping rather than discovered afterwards.

Straight answers

No. We are based in Faridabad, Haryana, India, and work with UAE clients remotely. There is no local office, no local team and no local entity.
